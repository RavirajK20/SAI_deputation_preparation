# Module 0 — Interview Blueprint

Source: Vacancy Notification F.No. 01-04001(03)/1/2026-66/411, dated 29.07.2026 (SAI, Human Resources Division-II). Verified directly from the original PDF — not a secondary summary.

## The post
- Assistant Director, Pay Level-10 (7th CPC), on deputation
- 9 posts (tentative)
- Deputation term: 1 year, extendable up to 3 years in one-year cycles, subject to requirement/vacancy availability
- Posting is All-India, wherever SAI's schemes are being implemented — the candidate does not choose location
- Age limit: max 56 years as on the date of receipt of application

## Eligibility (either route)
1. Already holding an analogous Assistant Director/Level-10 post in parent cadre/department, OR
2. At least 3 years regular service in Pay Matrix Level-7 (₹44,900–142,400)

## Selection process — stated plainly in the circular
"Candidates fulfilling eligibility criteria will be called for interview. Selection will be made on the basis of merit list prepared by the Selection Committee constituted by the Competent Authority."
No written exam mentioned anywhere. Eligibility screening → interview → merit list.

## The 11 duties (verbatim structure from the circular, English text)
i. Policy Implementation — oversee/ensure effective implementation of Govt policies, schemes, programmes in assigned domain
ii. Supervisory Role — supervise Section Officers/subordinate staff, timely disposal of files/cases
iii. Administrative Management — day-to-day departmental administrative functions: planning, coordination, monitoring
iv. File Examination & Decision Support — examine complex files/notes/proposals, provide recommendations, forward to higher authority
v. Subject-Specific Responsibilities — handle specialised areas: finance, HR, procurement, infrastructure, planning, legal, or technical functions depending on org structure
vi. Inter-Departmental Coordination — liaise with other departments, field units, external agencies
vii. Monitoring & Reporting — prepare reports, presentations, policy briefs; monitor scheme/administrative progress
viii. Compliance & Governance — ensure actions/decisions follow Govt rules, regulations, procedural norms
ix. Representation & Communication — represent org in meetings/committees/workshops, communicate decisions
x. Capacity Building & Institutional Support — contribute to org development, support training/review/reform
xi. Any other administrative task assigned by Competent Authority/Reporting Officer/Head of Division

## What this tells us about what's actually being tested
The duty list is overwhelmingly administrative/generalist, not sports-technical. Nowhere does it require coaching qualifications, sports-science background, or prior sports-federation experience. This is a Government-administration post located inside a sports organisation — the interview is far more likely to probe file-handling judgement, financial/procurement literacy, coordination ability, and compliance instinct than sports trivia.

## Enclosures required (all administrative, nothing sports-specific)
- Application in prescribed format (Annexure-I), countersigned by Cadre/Appointing Authority
- Attested ACRs/APARs for last 5 years (Under Secretary rank or above)
- Integrity Certificate
- Vigilance Clearance
- No major/minor penalty certificate (last 5 years)
- Certificate verifying particulars against service records
- Cadre Clearance Certificate

## CV Proforma note (Annexure-I, Q23)
"Experience in Sports Administration/Sports Achievement" is field 23, under an "Additional Information" section — informational, not an eligibility gate. A "Nil" answer here does not disqualify a candidate; it does mean any such experience has to be established verbally at interview, since nothing in the written record backs it up.

## Implicit competencies being tested (inferred from the duty list, not stated in the circular)
- Judgement under Government rules (file examination, recommendations)
- Financial/procurement literacy (GFR, GeM, delegation of powers)
- Cross-functional coordination
- Communication/representation ability
- Capacity to absorb a new technical domain fast (since "subject-specific" responsibilities vary by org need)
