# SAI Assistant Director — Deputation Interview Preparation

A self-contained study system for the SAI/MYAS Assistant Director deputation process: reference
notes on the actual institutional machinery, a narrative learning artifact that walks the same
material through scenes, full audio narration, animated chapter adaptations, and a recall/quiz
layer — all in one repository, deployed as a static site via GitHub Pages.

**Live site:** https://ravirajk20.github.io/SAI_deputation_preparation/

## Study modes

- **Learn** — the [Visual Systems Handbook](10-handbook/index.html) (institutional architecture as
  reference), [The Long Run](11-story/index.html) (a 13-chapter interactive narrative — fictional
  athlete and Assistant Director threads walking through the real institutional system), and the
  [audio study player](09-audio/index.html) (per-module Listening Room plus a single continuous
  25:33 full-story audiobook).
- **Watch** — 12 of the story's 13 narrative units are also produced as short painterly/illustrated
  animated chapters (~22.6 minutes total, linked from the [home page](index.html) and in
  [`video/`](video/)). The prologue chapter ("The Cast & The Map") is a reference/cast section, not
  a narrative scene, and was intentionally not produced as video — it's covered by the full-story
  audiobook instead. See [`video/PRODUCTION_STATUS.md`](video/PRODUCTION_STATUS.md) for the current
  status and known limitations.
- **Practice** — the [Recall Bench](quiz/index.html), a mobile-first quiz/recall app (Quick 10,
  Facts, Abbreviations, Divisions, Module Recall, Fact Traps, Weak Areas, Board Pressure, Explain
  Aloud).
- **Reference** — Modules 0–8: the interview blueprint, sports governance architecture, Khelo
  Bharat Niti-2025, the National Sports Governance Act 2025, SAI's organisation and machinery, the
  NCOE/STC/TOPS talent pipeline, SAI Annual Report findings, the 369th Parliamentary Standing
  Committee report, and a quick-reference layer (factsheets, fact traps, abbreviations, key dates,
  memory cards).

## Evidence discipline

Every module, the story, and the handbook use the same source-tagging convention: **Official
Fact**, **Parliamentary** (findings from the 369th Standing Committee), **Independent Evaluation**
(e.g. NIPFP), **Inference** (a reasonable but unconfirmed connection, stated as such), and, in the
story specifically, **Illustrative Scenario** and **Fictional**. Characters and invented scenes in
the story and its animated chapters are fictional; the institutions, rules, figures, and findings
they encounter are real and cited at first appearance. Nothing in this repository presents an
inference or an illustrative scenario as settled fact, and the story does not invent institutional
relationships that aren't documented.

This is independently compiled study material, not an official SAI/MYAS publication.
