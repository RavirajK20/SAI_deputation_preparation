## The chain of authority
Government of India
 → Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS)
 → Department of Sports
 → Sports Authority of India (SAI)

`[OFFICIAL-HISTORICAL]` SAI is an autonomous body, successor organisation of the IXth Asian Games (New Delhi, 1982), registered as a Society under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, per Resolution No. 1-1/83/SAI dated 25 January 1984 — verified directly from SAI's own site and the vacancy circular's "About SAI" section (both independently checked, word-for-word consistent).

## Institutional roles — corrected framing
Not "SAI = implementation arm" (too flat). More accurate:
- **MYAS/Department of Sports** — policy, programme design, funding allocation, governmental oversight of the whole ecosystem
- **SAI** — a major autonomous institution in its own right: has its own schemes, finances, 19 internal divisions, centres, and governance structure (General Body, Governing Body, Finance Committee) — implements Government sports policy but is not merely an execution unit
- **States/UTs** — critical implementation partners, especially for infrastructure (often co-funding above the central grant ceiling)
- **NSFs** — sport-specific governance and competition bodies, subject to MYAS recognition/suspension
- **Specialised institutions** — anti-doping (NADA/NDTL), education/research (National Sports University), funding (NSDF), dispute resolution (National Sports Tribunal), standards (National Sports Board)

## The authority-vs-function lens (apply this to every scenario question)
For any problem, ask five questions before answering:
1. Who owns the **policy**?
2. Who provides the **money**?
3. Who **executes**?
4. Who **monitors**?
5. Who has final **decision-making authority**, and who receives the **escalation**?

Example: "An NSF isn't complying with governance requirements" — policy/standard-setting sits with the National Sports Board (NSGA-2025), not SAI; SAI would only be involved if the NSF also runs SAI-linked athlete programmes. Getting this chain right is more valuable than reciting scheme facts.

## `[OFFICIAL-CURRENT]` SAI's 19 organisational divisions — independently verified directly from SAI's own site (Organisational Information → Divisions tab, checked via live page content, not a secondary citation)
TEAMS, TOPS, IT, Sports Science, Stadia, Engineering, Capacity Building, Finance, Coordination, General Administration & Procurement, Legal, Vigilance, Media, Rajbhasha, Khelo India, Operations, HR, Policy/Research & Innovation, TAGG.

**IT Division function — independently confirmed, not inflated:** develops/maintains NSRS (National Sports Repository System), Khelo India website/mobile app, Fitness App, SAI facility online-booking; manages cloud infrastructure and security (with NIC); runs e-Office Premium, SPARROW, and file/leave/tour/personnel modules; maintains LAN/WAN across HQ, JLN Stadium, and Regional Centres; and explicitly runs a **"Data Analytics and Sports Intelligence"** function — athlete tracking, performance benchmarking, AI/ML-based insights, integrated dashboards for decision-making.

`[INTERVIEW PREP]` Implication already established in the candidate-evidence work: do not claim SAI lacks digital/data infrastructure — it doesn't. The credible position is understanding and strengthening an existing system, not introducing one.

## `[OFFICIAL-CURRENT]` Other institutions
- **NSDF** — blends Government funding with CSR/private philanthropy (PSU contributions ~₹262.3 crore per NIPFP evaluation); primary financing backbone for TOPS
- **National Sports Board** and **National Sports Tribunal** — newly constituted under the National Sports Governance Act, 2025; both have 2026 Rules notified (recruitment, service conditions, search-cum-selection committee rules) — see Module 2
- **NADA/NDTL** — anti-doping testing and enforcement; WADA sets the international code

## Who does what — summary table
| Function | Owner |
|---|---|
| Policy | MYAS/Department of Sports |
| Funding | MYAS budget + NSDF (blended) + State co-funding |
| Implementation | SAI + State Sports Departments + NSFs (competitions) |
| Governance/regulation | National Sports Board; NSFs' own governance under NSGA-2025 standards |
| Dispute resolution | National Sports Tribunal |
| Anti-doping | NADA/NDTL (testing), WADA (code) |
| Oversight | Parliament (Standing Committee), CAG/audit, RTI |
