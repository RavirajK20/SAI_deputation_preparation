# Module 1 — India's Sports Governance Architecture

## The chain of authority
Government of India
 → Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS)
 → Department of Sports
 → Sports Authority of India (SAI) — autonomous body registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, mandate: development and promotion of sport, achieving excellence, preparing sportspersons for international competition. (Verified directly from the vacancy circular's own "About SAI" section.)

## Key institutions and what each actually does
- **SAI** — implementation arm: runs Regional Centres, NCOEs, STCs, NSTC, schemes (Khelo India delivery, TOPS support), infrastructure, sports science
- **National Sports Federations (NSFs)** — sport-specific governance and competition structure; subject to recognition/suspension by MYAS (e.g., Table Tennis Federation of India recognition was suspended, per MYAS notice 12-Aug-2026 — current, not historical)
- **IOA** — Indian Olympic movement
- **NADA / NDTL** — anti-doping testing and enforcement
- **NSDF** — National Sports Development Fund; blends Government funding with CSR/private philanthropy (PSU contributions ~₹262.3 crore per NIPFP evaluation); primary financing backbone for TOPS
- **National Sports University** — sports education/research
- **National Sports Board** and **National Sports Tribunal** — newly constituted under the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 (see Module 2); both have 2026 Rules notified (recruitment, service conditions, search-cum-selection committee rules)
- **States/UTs** — implementation partners, especially for Khelo India infrastructure (state contribution often required above the central grant ceiling)

## Who does what (the distinction to keep straight in interview answers)
- **Policy**: MYAS/Department of Sports
- **Funding**: MYAS budget + NSDF (blended public/private) + State co-funding
- **Implementation**: SAI (centres, schemes) + State Sports Departments + NSFs (competitions)
- **Governance/regulation**: National Sports Board, NSFs' own governance (now under NSGA-2025 standards)
- **Dispute resolution**: National Sports Tribunal
- **Anti-doping**: NADA/NDTL (testing), WADA (international code)
- **Oversight**: Parliament (Standing Committee reports), CAG/audit, RTI

## SAI's actual organisational divisions (verified from SAI's own site)
Operations, Khelo India, TOPS, TEAMS, TAGG, Sports Science, IT, Stadia, Engineering, Capacity Building, Finance, Coordination, General Administration & Procurement, Legal, Vigilance, HR, Policy/Research & Innovation, Media, Rajbhasha.

## Why this matters for the interview
A credible AD answer should be able to place any scenario correctly in this chain — e.g., "an NSF governance complaint" is a National Sports Board/NSGA-2025 matter, not something SAI administers directly; "a KIC not meeting its trainee quota" is a SAI Operations/Khelo India Division and State Sports Department coordination matter, not a Ministry-level one. Knowing *who is actually responsible* for a given problem is more valuable than knowing scheme trivia.
