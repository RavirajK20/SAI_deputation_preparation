# Interview Numbers Bank

## MUST KNOW
- SAI established 1984; 19 divisions
- 24 NCOEs approved / 22 operational (2023-24) — the gap itself is the point to remember
- 69 STCs, 26 Extension Centres
- ~45% SAI-wide post vacancy (Parliamentary Committee) — do not confuse with the 56% KISCE figure
- Khelo India ~67% of Dept. of Sports FY25-26 budget
- NSGA-2025: enacted 18.08.2025, commencement staged (31.12.2025 then 12.05.2026)

## GOOD TO KNOW
- 187 total SAI centres, 9,432 athletes system-wide
- ₹38.75 crore FY23-24 procurement; ₹21.8 crore stadia revenue
- 814 total coaches (476 regular / 308 contract / 30 deputation)
- 1,067 notified Khelo India Centres, 991 operational
- 328 sanctioned Khelo India infra projects, 74% complete

## DO NOT MEMORISE (low interview value — interesting, not useful)
- Individual stadium seating capacities
- Specific procurement contract values for minor items (sarees, statuettes, etc.)
- Historical Governing Body meeting numbers/dates beyond the current cycle
- Detailed academic course enrolment counts at NSNIS/LNCPE
