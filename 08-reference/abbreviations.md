# Abbreviation Dictionary

*Full form + one-line meaning + why it matters for an AD interview. Verified against Modules 0–7 — not expanded from memory.*

## Institutions / Government
- **MYAS** — Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — parent Ministry
- **SAI** — Sports Authority of India — the employer
- **DoS** — Department of Sports — the Ministry wing SAI sits under
- **NSGA** — National Sports Governance Act, 2025 — new statutory governance framework
- **NSB** — National Sports Board — new standards/regulatory body under NSGA
- **NST** — National Sports Tribunal — new dispute-resolution body under NSGA
- **NSFs** — National Sports Federations — sport-specific governing bodies
- **IOA** — Indian Olympic Association
- **IPC** — Indian Paralympic Committee
- **NADA** — National Anti-Doping Agency — testing/enforcement
- **NDTL** — National Dope Testing Laboratory
- **NSDF** — National Sports Development Fund — blended public/CSR funding, primary TOPS financing (flagged in the 369th Committee report for fund-utilisation concerns)

## SAI programmes/schemes
- **NCOE** — National Centre of Excellence — high-performance training hub
- **STC** — SAI Training Centre — junior-level development, ages 10–18
- **NSTC** — National Sports Talent Contest — school-based scouting, ages 8–14
- **TOPS** — Target Olympic Podium Scheme — elite medal-prospect support
- **TEAMS** — Training of Elite Athletes & Management Support
- **TAGG** — Target Asian Games Group
- **KIC** — Khelo India Centre
- **KISCE** — Khelo India State Centre of Excellence
- **KI** — Khelo India (the umbrella scheme)

## Digital/administration
- **NSRS** — National Sports Repository System — SAI's central athlete/data platform
- **SPARROW** — Smart Performance Appraisal Report Recording Online Window — APAR system
- **PFMS** — Public Financial Management System
- **GeM** — Government e-Marketplace — procurement platform
- **GFR** — General Financial Rules — governs Government procurement/expenditure
- **CVC** — Central Vigilance Commission
- **RTI** — Right to Information
- **APAR** — Annual Performance Appraisal Report (successor to ACR)
- **ACR** — Annual Confidential Report (older term for APAR)
- **UC** — Utilisation Certificate — proof of how a grant/fund was spent, recurring theme in NIPFP findings

## Sports science/performance
- **MOC** — Mission Olympic Cell — the body administering TOPS, chaired by DG SAI
- **HPD** — High Performance Director — senior technical role at NCOEs

## Policy/governance
- **Khelo Bharat Niti** — National Sports Policy-2025
- **NEP** — National Education Policy, 2020 — Pillar 5 of Khelo Bharat Niti aligns with it
