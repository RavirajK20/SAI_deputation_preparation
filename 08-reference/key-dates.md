# Key Dates

*Verified only — nothing added for length.*

- **1982** — IX Asian Games, New Delhi (SAI's origin point)
- **25.01.1984** — SAI established (Resolution No. 1-1/83/SAI)
- **01.05.1987** — SNIPES (incl. NSNIS Patiala) merged into SAI
- **Sept 1995** — LNCPE Gwalior delinked, became a deemed university
- **18.09.2019** — MYAS OM No. K-11020/4/2019-Sports-V — NCOE restructuring/merger decision
- **13.07.2020** — SAI RC Kandivali, Mumbai renamed SAI Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee NCOE, Mumbai
- **16.11.2022** — SAI General Body reconstituted
- **01.09.2023** — SAI Governing Body reconstituted
- **01.07.2025** — Khelo Bharat Niti-2025 launched (corrected from an earlier wrongly-cited 10.07.2025)
- **18.08.2025** — National Sports Governance Act, 2025 enacted
- **20.08.2025** — 369th Parliamentary Standing Committee report presented
- **31.12.2025** — First NSGA commencement notification
- **01.01.2026** — Selected NSGA provisions commence
- **12.05.2026** — Second NSGA commencement notification (further provisions/rules)
- **29.07.2026** — SAI AD vacancy notification (F.No. 01-04001(03)/1/2026-66/411) issued
- **31.07.2026 – 30.08.2026** — SAI AD application window
- **March 2026 (target)** — Khelo India scheme's current sanctioned period ends per Committee report
