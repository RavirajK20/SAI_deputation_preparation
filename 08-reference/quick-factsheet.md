## SAI at a glance
- Established 1984 (Resolution No. 1-1/83/SAI, dated 25.01.1984), successor to the IXth Asian Games (Delhi, 1982)
- Society under the Societies Registration Act, 1860
- Autonomous body under Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS), Department of Sports
- SNIPES (incl. NSNIS Patiala) merged into SAI effective 1 May 1987; LNCPE Gwalior delinked in Sept 1995 (became a deemed university)
- Core mandate: promote sport, achieve sporting excellence, prepare athletes for international competition
- Governance: General Body (36 members, reconstituted 16.11.2022) + Governing Body (28 members, reconstituted 01.09.2023) + Finance Committee; chaired by the Union Minister
- 19 organisational divisions (see one-line sheet) — note structure has evolved between the AR 2023-24 org chart and the current live site; don't assume static
