# Interview Fact Traps — Don't Say This

### DO NOT SAY: "SAI lacks digital infrastructure."
**SAFE:** "SAI already has substantial digital infrastructure, including NSRS and data-analytics/AI-ML capabilities. Documented evaluations identify implementation and user-adoption issues in some systems, not an absence of systems."

### DO NOT SAY: "NSGA replaced SAI."
**SAFE:** "NSGA establishes a statutory governance/regulatory framework for sports bodies generally. SAI continues as the sports-development implementation institution under MYAS — they're not the same thing."

### DO NOT SAY: "NSGA fully came into force on 1 January 2026."
**SAFE:** "Selected provisions commenced from 1 January 2026, with further provisions and rules notified afterward, including in May 2026 — implementation has been staged, not a single cutover."

### DO NOT SAY: "SAI's 2024-25 Annual Report shows..."
**SAFE:** "The most recent SAI Annual Report I've verified is 2023-24." (No 2024-25 report could be located anywhere — don't improvise its contents.)

### DO NOT SAY: "The Mission Olympic Cell meets weekly."
No primary source confirms meeting frequency. If asked, say frequency isn't something you've confirmed rather than guessing.

### DO NOT MERGE: SAI-wide vacancy (~45%, Parliamentary Committee) with KISCE manpower fill rate (56% average, NIPFP).
These measure different populations at different scopes — conflating them under interview pressure is an easy, avoidable mistake.

### DO NOT SAY: "I know who the current DG SAI is."
That post's current holder is explicitly unconfirmed in this repo (see important-persons.md). Don't guess a name under pressure — say you'd verify rather than state one incorrectly.

### DO NOT SAY (unprompted): anything editorialising on the NSDF fund-diversion finding.
If it comes up, state it as the Committee's own finding plainly ("the Committee flagged a need for stricter NSDF regulation") — don't speculate about wrongdoing beyond what the report itself says.

### DO NOT SAY: "The vacancies were created because of Khelo Bharat Niti-2025 / NSGA-2025."
No source establishes that causal link for this specific AD vacancy batch. If asked why now, describe the policies as current context, not as the stated cause of this recruitment.

### DO NOT SAY: "Manjeet Singh Chahal reached the national level in hockey."
(Personal-evidence trap, not institutional — included here because it's the same discipline.) He's a track athlete (800m/1500m, Asian Games gold 2018), not hockey.
