## Permanent caveat 2 — the required framing for the 6B-4 null result

The 0/16 result must never be presented as evidence that government support produced no outcomes.
The user's specified wording for any future presentation of this finding:

> "No qualifying within-window co-occurrences were confirmed in the lag-eligible population under
> the project's strict dating rules. This should not be interpreted as evidence that government
> support was followed by no outcomes; the available dates generally cannot establish when support
> began."

The reason this framing is required, not just preferred: only 2 of the 16 Pop-C athletes have an
actual-date anchor at all, and both anchors are later re-addition events that postdate the
athlete's major-games results — meaning the test's null result reflects a dating-evidence gap, not
an absence of any real temporal relationship.

## Permanent caveat 3 — the A/B/C distinction, never to be conflated

Three genuinely different findings came out of this project and must stay labeled as such in every
future stage, including the interview-preparation and any presentation stage:

- **A. Descriptive evidence exists** — a substantial government athlete-support register and
  observable competitive outcomes exist and were assembled with source-tier discipline.
- **B. A particular association test returned zero confirmed co-occurrences** — the strict Pop-C +
  fixed-lag test (6B-4) produced 0/16, for the dating-evidence reason in Caveat 2.
- **C. The intended financial support → outcome relationship is not estimable** — 6B-5 found zero
  eligible financially-timed observations; this is a source-data observability finding, not a null
  effect finding.

**B must never be presented as C, and C must never be presented as "government support doesn't
work."** The absence of a usable causal/financial relationship is itself a finding about public-data
observability — a legitimate and reportable substantive result in its own right, not a project
failure to be minimized or a policy verdict to be inferred from.
