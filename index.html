Evidence discipline

Every module, the story, and the handbook use the same source-tagging convention throughout this project:
Official FactParliamentaryIndependent Evaluation InferenceIllustrative ScenarioFictional (story only)
In the story and its animated chapters specifically: characters and invented scenes are fictional; the institutions, rules, figures, and findings they encounter are real and cited at first appearance. Nothing in this repository states an inference or an illustrative scenario as settled fact.
